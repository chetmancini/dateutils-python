Direction 02: a minimal lowercase d-shaped orbit/time dial symbol with one amber circular satellite; ink navy and amber.
Direction 03: a compact grid of rounded square day cells with five active green cells forming a forward stepped path and two muted cells suggesting weekends; forest green.
Wordmarks: highly legible contemporary geometric sans, beautifully kerned, no italics. Flat vector-like logo treatment, crisp edges, no dimensionality, gradients, mockups or shadows. These are three distinct usable mark + wordmark concepts, not app screens. No Python snake logo. No additional text or watermarks.
